# AI assistance

AI tools may assist with implementation, review, documentation, and test design.
Every contribution remains the responsibility of the human author and must pass
the same review and validation requirements regardless of how it was produced.

Do not add a `Co-authored-by` trailer for an AI system. Use the pull request
description to disclose material AI assistance when it helps reviewers evaluate
the work.

AI-generated financial claims and sources are untrusted until independently
verified. Never fabricate evidence to make an audit pass.
